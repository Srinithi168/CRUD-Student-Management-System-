Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about LearnHub.

What is LearnHub?

LearnHub is an online learning platform where students can access courses, track progress, and earn certificates.

Are the courses free?

Many beginner courses are free, while advanced courses may require enrollment.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. After completing a course successfully, you can download a certificate.

Can I learn on mobile?

Yes. LearnHub is fully responsive and works on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

How do I contact support?

You can use the Contact page or email us at support@learnhub.com.
